About Mogzy Discovery

Find fan-made League of Legends websites, tools and games. Researched from public pages and hands-on tests. No paid placement, no rankings.

What Mogzy Discovery is

Mogzy Discovery is an atlas of independent League of Legends fan websites, tools and games. We find them, describe them in plain words and link to the original sites. We do not host or copy them.

Our method is simple: we read public pages, record what a site says about itself, and for some categories we run hands-on tests. Everything on a page shows how we know it.

Tested, observed and claimed

Tested means we ran it ourselves and recorded the result in a single session. The test date and the patch the tool showed are noted.

Observed means we saw it on the site, such as a feature, a price or a patch label, without running a structured test.

Claimed means the site says so and we have not confirmed it. We never upgrade the wording: a claim stays a claim until we have seen or tested it.

Independence and no paid placement

Nobody can pay to be listed, moved up or described more kindly. We do not take payment or free access in exchange for coverage, and listings are not ordered by any score.

Mogzy also builds its own tools. Those appear on this site labelled Mogzy original, with a note that we made them, and are never described as better than independent tools.

What we never publish

We keep our working notes private and publish only what is meant for visitors. We do not publish private notes or reviews, personal contact details, or internal assessments of any person or site.

Where information about a site is not known, we say it is not known. Missing is never shown as zero or as a negative.

Corrections and removal requests

If something about your project is wrong, out of date or unfair, or you would like it removed, tell us through mogzy.lol and say which page you mean. We will check and correct it, and we will remove a listing at the owner's request.

How the data updates

The data refreshes automatically as new research is added, so pages can change without notice. Each page shows when it was last updated.

We check whether sites can be reached from time to time. If a site could not be reached when we last checked, we say so with the date. That is not proof it is gone, since sites go down briefly and return.
