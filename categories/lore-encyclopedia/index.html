League of Legends lore and encyclopedias

Lore sites collect the stories, regions, characters and timeline of the League universe, often with a searchable wiki format.

Good lore references distinguish canon from fan theory and link back to the source story. Check how often the site is updated after new releases.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
