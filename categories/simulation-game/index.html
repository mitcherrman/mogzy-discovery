League of Legends simulation and management games

Simulation games let you run a team, manage a career, play out a season or build a roguelike run using League's champions and esports world.

Look at how long a session takes, whether progress is saved locally or to an account, and whether the rosters and rules reflect the current game or an earlier season.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.

6 sites in this category.

  • DraftCup — Free browser Worlds simulator: draft real pro players, simulate Swiss and playoffs, duel friends; includes a Worlds database of history and standings. (Simulation or management game, Esports data and records)
  • Leyenda de la Grieta — Spanish-language pro-career simulation: play a pro player from debut at 18 to retirement at 21 across 3-4 seasons; finals are played via 13 skill minigames, 81 decision events, Worlds/MSI/First Stand/Olympics, teammates, a daily seeded 'Carrera del Dia' and a shareable retirement… (Fan game, Simulation or management game)
  • LOL PERFECT WORLDS (Simulation or management game, Esports data and records)
  • Nasus Q Farming Simulator (Fan game, Simulation or management game)
  • Solo Queue — Ranked-climb strategy game for ScoreSpace Jam #13: choose a role, queue, lock in a champion and try to raise Elo before the 'season ends in 6 hours'; page has a Support This Game option. (Fan game, Simulation or management game)
  • Worldslike — Esports roguelike (Simulation or management game, Esports data and records)
