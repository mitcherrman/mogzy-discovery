League of Legends esports data and records

These sites collect match results, player and team histories, rosters, standings and records from professional League of Legends.

Coverage varies by league, region and year. Look for a clear statement of what is included, how often it updates and where the data comes from, and watch for gaps in older seasons.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
