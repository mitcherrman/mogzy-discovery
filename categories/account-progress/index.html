League of Legends account progress trackers

Account progress tools show your champion mastery, challenge progress, hours played and season recaps in one place.

They usually ask for your Riot ID or a sign-in, so check what they ask for, what they store and which regions they support.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.

4 sites in this category.

  • Challenges.lol — Challenge and mastery tracker: search a Riot ID for challenge progress and champion mastery, with Challenges, Leaderboards, Compare, Team Builder, Getting Started and 'Crystal' pages. (Account progress)
  • ChampionMastery.gg — Champion-mastery highscore site: global and per-region top-3 lists for total points, total level and each champion's mastery points, plus player lookup over 17 regions. (Account progress)
  • League Checker — Browser champion tracker for Classic, Arena and ARAM completion. (Account progress)
  • RiftMastery — Meta description: track League of Legends champion mastery, mastery points, official mastery challenges and role progression by Riot ID. (Account progress, Player stats and profiles)
