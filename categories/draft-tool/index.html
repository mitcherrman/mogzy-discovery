League of Legends draft tools

Draft tools let you practise champion select: ban and pick order, team composition and sometimes fearless or tournament draft rules. Some suggest picks, and others simply give you a board to plan on.

Check which draft format it supports, whether champion data and roles are current, whether you can draft with friends or against an AI, and whether the suggestions say where they come from.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
