League of Legends randomizers

Randomizers pick champions, items, runes or challenge rules at random to make games interesting. Many are used for custom games and challenge runs.

Look at what you can filter, such as roles, owned champions or banned picks, and whether the pool includes the latest champions.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.

8 sites in this category.

  • Elo.rip — Champion randomizer: spin for a random champion, filter by role or ARAM, build custom pools, and play a daily champion guessing game (from the page title and meta description; the page body is client-rendered). (Randomizer)
  • Hex Fuser — Hex Fuser: League randomizer rolling champion, position, skin, summoner spells, items (physical/magic filters), runes, challenge and ward skin, with exclusions and click-to-select; claims data kept current with each patch and multiple League languages; cross-promotes kkmet, lolsk… (Randomizer)
  • LoL Custom Randomizer — Free fan tool for League custom games: balanced teams, roles, champion draws, builds and runes. (Randomizer)
  • LoL Wheel (Randomizer)
  • Mikaela Lundstrom Champion Randomizer — Web champion and skin randomizer with role filter and custom pool. (Randomizer)
  • RandTools (Randomizer)
  • Team Roulette — Static-file League custom-game randomizer (team split plus champion roulette) hosted on a personal project page. (Randomizer)
  • Ultimate Bravery — Random-build challenge site: rolls a champion, role, item order, summoner spells, runes and ability max order for Summoner's Rift (Live and Classic modes, also Wild Rift and TFT tabs) with seven self-enforced rules and shareable builds; also links LoLGuessr. (Randomizer)
