League of Legends matchup tools

Matchup tools describe how one champion fares into another, one-on-one or in team fights, using win rates, tips or community knowledge.

The key questions are where the numbers come from, how many games they cover, which patch and rank range they reflect, and whether they separate data from opinion.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
