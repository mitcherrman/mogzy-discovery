League of Legends meta stats and tier lists

Meta sites publish champion win rates, pick rates, ban rates and tier lists, usually by patch, rank and role.

Compare the rank range and sample size behind the numbers, the patch they cover, and whether the site explains how a tier is decided. A tier list is a summary of a method, so it is worth knowing the method.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
