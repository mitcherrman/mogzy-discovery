League of Legends mechanics trainers

Mechanics trainers are small practice tools for the physical side of the game, such as dodging skillshots, kiting, last hitting and reaction time.

They are not the real game, so check how closely timings and ranges match it and whether the tool states which patch it follows. Many run in the browser, but input feel can differ on a phone or a laptop trackpad.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
