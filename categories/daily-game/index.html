League of Legends daily games

A daily game gives every player the same puzzle once a day, usually guessing a champion, ability, skin or item from a handful of clues, then resets at a fixed time. They are quick, social and easy to share.

Things worth checking: when the puzzle resets and in which time zone, whether your streak is saved without an account, how the clues are chosen, and whether the game keeps up with new champions after a patch.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.

8 sites in this category.

  • Champdle — Daily League quiz game with Quadra (grouping), Imposter (odd ability) and Guess-by-build modes plus player-made boards. (Daily game, Quiz and trivia)
  • LoLdle — Daily League of Legends champion guessing site with five modes (Classic, Quote, Ability, Emoji, Splash), a temporary 'Worlds Mayhem' mode ('Build your dream roster, win the cup'), and links to sister games for other franchises (Pokedle, Dotadle, Smashdle, OnePiecedle, Narutodle). (Daily game, Guessing game)
  • LoLdoku — Independent free fan project: daily and unlimited League champion puzzle modes (Grid, Classic, Silhouette, Attributes, Abilities, Connections); not affiliated with Riot Games. (Daily game, Guessing game)
  • lols.gg — Daily champion puzzle (#126 in retrieved page) (Daily game)
  • Loredia — Six daily games; daily rank points (Daily game)
  • QWERmix — Champion creation, daily draft, team comp (Daily game, Draft tool, Fan game)
  • RiftGuessr — Daily champion guessing (Daily game, Guessing game)
