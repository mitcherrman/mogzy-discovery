League of Legends replay and VOD analysis tools

Replay and VOD tools help you review games: tagging moments, tracking timelines, annotating video and comparing play to known patterns.

Look at what files or accounts the tool needs, what it stores, and whether it works from your own recordings.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.

3 sites in this category.

  • MidDiff (Meta statistics, Coaching and education, Replay and VOD analysis)
  • Replay.gg — League replay service (2016): servers automatically record every game of a signed-up summoner; search a summoner name and watch replays of recent games without installing replay software; regions NA, EUW, EUNE, OCE, BR, LAN, LAS, KR. (Replay and VOD analysis)
  • RoflLab — Replay-analysis tool for League teams and solo-queue players: upload .rofl files from scrims and official matches, it recognises every player (even on smurfs), compares each with their own role and shows what is more than luck; free per meta description, login required, live demo… (Replay and VOD analysis)
