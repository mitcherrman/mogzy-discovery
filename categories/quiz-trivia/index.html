League of Legends quizzes and trivia

Quizzes and trivia sites test what you know about champions, lore, items, esports history and game mechanics, usually as multiple choice or short answers.

Good quizzes cite where their answers come from and say when they were last checked. Some questions go out of date after a patch, so look for a note about the game version.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
