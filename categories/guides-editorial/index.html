League of Legends guides and articles

Guides and editorial sites publish champion guides, patch breakdowns, analysis and news written by fans and independent writers.

Check the date on each article and the patch it describes. Good guides say what they assume and link to their sources.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
