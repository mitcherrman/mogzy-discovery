League of Legends guessing games

Guessing games ask you to identify a champion, ability, item, skin or rank from limited clues. Unlike daily games, many let you keep playing as long as you like.

When choosing one, look at what it can quiz you on, whether it has new champions and items, whether it works on a phone, and whether it asks you to sign in to keep a score.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
