League of Legends build tools

Build tools help you plan items, runes and stats for a champion, from simple item sets to calculators that show total stats and cost.

Look for the patch the data comes from, whether the tool recalculates stats as you change items, whether you can save or share a build, and whether it needs an account.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
