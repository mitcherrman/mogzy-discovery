League of Legends fan games

Fan games are original games made in or inspired by League of Legends, such as roguelikes, card games, platformers and tactics games. They are separate projects, not official Riot products.

Before you play, check whether the game runs in the browser or needs a download, whether it is finished or still in development, how it treats your save data, and how much of it relies on Riot's characters and art.

Each listing on Mogzy Discovery links to the site and records what it says it does, whether it is free, which patch it shows (when it shows one), and whether we tested it, only looked at it, or are repeating its own claims.
