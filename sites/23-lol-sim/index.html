LoL Sim

LoL Sim is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Combat and damage calculator. We tested its DPS / trial simulation and displays supported patch. In one test session we ran 9 checks: 8 passed and 1 failed. The earliest public record we found dates from September 2026.

Visit lolsim.io

Combat and damage calculator
