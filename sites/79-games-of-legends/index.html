Games of Legends

Games of Legends is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Esports data and records. The site says it offers optional account, esports records and esports stats pages. In one test session we ran 10 checks: 9 passed and 1 partly passed.

Visit gol.gg

How the site describes itself

Games of Legends: 'The home of League of Legends competitive statistics' with pro game statistics; sections Tournaments, Teams, Players, Champions, Plays, Tools and Premium, plus search by team, player or champion. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
