Poro.gg

Poro.gg is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Meta statistics. We saw optional account. The site says it offers champion win/pick/ban statistics, champion tier list, champion patch change history and ladder tracking.

Korean-origin stats site (KR default) with champion tiers for Summoner's Rift, ARAM, URF and other game modes, patch champion-change lists (v26.23), masters/leaderboards, mythic stats, favorites, multi-search and Riot sign-in. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
