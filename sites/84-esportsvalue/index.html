EsportsValue

EsportsValue is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Esports data and records. The site says it offers multiple daily modes, player market values, roster builder and roster and transfer tracking. In one test session we ran 12 checks: 8 passed and 3 partly passed.

Visit esportsvalue.com

How the site describes itself

League of Legends esports hub: market values, stats, contracts, transfers and match schedule for pros; includes value movers, player comparison, watchlist, squad builder and daily games (Quizie, Higher or Lower, Guess the Value). (the site says, 2026-11-25)
