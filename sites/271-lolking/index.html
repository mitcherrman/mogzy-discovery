LoLKing

LoLKing is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Data archive and Player stats and profiles. We saw shareable item builds, 3D model viewer, leaderboard and summoner lookup. The earliest public record we found dates from January 2012.

Visit www.lolking.net

About

2017: full-featured stats and guides site (LolKing): summoner stats in 12 server groups, champion guides (popular/new/highly rated), charts, 3D models, leaderboards, replays, streams, login/premium; live URL now redirects to a Fanbyte legacy 'League of Legends Model Viewer' article page. (seen on the site, 2026-11-25)
