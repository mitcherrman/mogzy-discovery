Rift Data 峡谷数据

Rift Data 峡谷数据 is a mobile app for League of Legends players, listed under Other. We saw iOS app. The site says it offers no account required, rune and item recommendations, schedules and calendars and matchup statistics.

Visit apps.apple.com

How the site describes itself

Free iOS/iPadOS app on the China App Store (title 峡谷数据 - 上分有迹可循, 'Real-time records, champion strength, ranked analysis'): champion win rates, builds and runes, counters and synergies by role and rank, summoner lookup with KDA and win rate, rank leaderboards, live-game lineups and LCK/LPL esports schedules; no sign-in; data from public APIs, unaffiliated with Riot. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
