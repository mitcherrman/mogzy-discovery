LoLMeta

LoLMeta is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Build tool and Meta statistics. We saw pro builds and pro pick stats and champion tier list.

Visit lolmeta.info

About

Japanese-language stats site for the current meta: champion pick/ban rates (1/3/7/14/30-day windows), win rates, pro-match data and builds; archived pages show it updating through 2026. (seen on the site, 2026-11-25)
