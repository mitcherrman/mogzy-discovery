uDrafter

uDrafter is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Draft tool. We tested its no account required, draft analysis or grade and draft trend analytics. The site says it offers optional account, ban phase support, best-of series drafting and collaborative drafting. In one test session we ran 2 checks: 1 passed and 1 partly passed.

Visit udrafter.eu

How the site describes itself

Free browser draft simulator and pick/ban tool that follows the official pick/ban order in real time, with Classic or Fearless series, shareable links with observer seats and a solo mode. Not affiliated with Riot Games. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Audience as stated or implied by the page: Scrim teams, coaches, casters and streamers; names Arrakis Esport and Olympus Esport as users (the site says, 2026-11-25)
