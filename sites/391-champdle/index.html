Champdle

Champdle is a web game for League of Legends players, listed under Daily game and Quiz and trivia. We tested its no account required and connections / grouping puzzle. We saw daily reset puzzle. The site says it offers user-made boards or puzzles, multiple daily modes, guess the build and odd ability out. In one test session we ran 3 checks: 1 passed and 2 partly passed. The earliest public record we found dates from June 2024.

Visit www.champdle.com

How the site describes itself

Daily League quiz game with Quadra (grouping), Imposter (odd ability) and Guess-by-build modes plus player-made boards. (the site says, 2026-11-24)
