Ultimate Bravery

Ultimate Bravery is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Randomizer. We saw no account required. The site says it offers random full build, ultimate Bravery-style challenge roll and share link for a plan or draft. The earliest public record we found dates from October 2018.

Visit ultimate-bravery.net

How the site describes itself

Random-build challenge site: rolls a champion, role, item order, summoner spells, runes and ability max order for Summoner's Rift (Live and Classic modes, also Wild Rift and TFT tabs) with seven self-enforced rules and shareable builds; also links LoLGuessr. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
