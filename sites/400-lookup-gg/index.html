Lookup.GG

Lookup.GG is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Player stats and profiles. The site says it offers friend comparison, ladder tracking, live game companion and match history and per-game detail.

Visit lookup.gg

How the site describes itself

Summoner lookup site (formerly lookup.ynk.me) currently showing 'Coming soon': a full rebuild planned with player profiles, complete match history, duo and teammate stats, ranked ladders, LP tracking and live-game ranks; a November 2026 note says it is moving to new servers.
