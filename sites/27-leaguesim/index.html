LeagueSim

LeagueSim is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Combat and damage calculator. We saw DPS / trial simulation and open source repository. The site says it offers combo damage calculation and rune optimiser.

Visit chaodhib.github.io

How the site describes itself

LeagueSim web app (GitHub Pages) with two modes, Simulation Mode and Item Optimizer: pick champion, target, runes, skill order, items and abilities, then run a simulation; results table lists damage, time, DPS, items, gold cost, ability sequence and whether it kills; CSV export.
