Arailla analytics

Arailla analytics is an open-source project for League of Legends players, listed under Meta statistics.

Visit github.com

How the site describes itself

Curated README of League data-analytics resources by 'Arailla': data sources (solo queue, static, esports, client data), libraries, tutorials, written pieces, videos/podcasts, GitHub repositories and Discord servers; contributions welcome by pull request. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Related sites

  • MidDiff (Meta statistics, Coaching and education, Replay and VOD analysis)
  • LoLalytics — Win-rate analytics site: tier list, champion builds/runes/counters, ARAM and Arena tier lists, pro builds (Pros.LoL) and patch champion performance for Patch 16.23, with a stated Emerald+ default bracket and 'analyse 100% of champions in a bracket' method. (Meta statistics)
  • League of Graphs — League of Graphs: champion and summoner statistics: most recent champion, most popular, best and most banned champions with popularity/win/ban rates, winrate gains and losses since a patch, best Arena trios, full champion list sortable by role; summoner search; ad slots with a 'R… (Meta statistics, Player stats and profiles)
  • NerfPlz — Long-running Blogger-based League guide site (copyright 2011-2026) with solo-queue tier lists, ARAM Mayhem tier list/builds/augments, 3D skin viewer, patch summaries and PBE changes, draft/team-comp/counter tools, trivia/music/lore map and game-help utilities. (Meta statistics)
  • Jungle Clear — Alpha-stage jungle coaching service: send one jungle game and receive an algorithmic (stated AI-free) analysis covering macro, objectives, tempo, ganks and clears, with an interactive demo; English and French only; 2 free analyses per day during alpha. (Meta statistics)
  • Doran's Lab — Undergraduate research lab (Laber Labs lineage, copyright 2018) publishing data-science articles and tools about League: Doran's Lab Manual, jungle path tool, ARAM win predictor, critical strike algorithm, Sion analysis, Nexus Blitz numbers. (Meta statistics)
