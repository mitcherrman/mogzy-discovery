Rift Rumble

Rift Rumble is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Competitions and tournaments. The site says it offers captain-draft league and open-registration tournament.

Visit www.riftrumble.net

How the site describes itself

Draft-based League of Legends community for amateur players: individuals sign up, approved captains draft teams; weekly Lower Elo (Bronze to low Platinum) and Higher Elo (high Platinum to Masters 200LP) matches, then playoffs and championship broadcasts. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Audience as stated or implied by the page: Amateur players in two Elo bands; region not stated (the site says, 2026-11-25)
