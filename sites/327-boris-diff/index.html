Boris Diff

Boris Diff is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Combat and damage calculator. We tested its combo damage calculation and displays supported patch. The site says it offers champion-specific calculator and item and rune build calculator. In one test session we ran 9 checks: 7 passed, 1 partly passed and 1 failed.

Visit borisdiff.gg

How the site describes itself

Boris Diff: League damage calculator; 'Build combos, compare damage, and theorycraft item builds'; champion picker currently lists Akali, Lux, Ahri, Katarina, Vayne, Jhin, Quinn and Locke with 'More champions coming soon' and a Discord vote for the next champion. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
