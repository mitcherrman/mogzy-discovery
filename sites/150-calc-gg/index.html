Calc.gg

Calc.gg is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Combat and damage calculator. The site says it offers sequential action queue, item and rune build calculator and displays supported patch.

How the site describes itself

calc.gg: League of Legends calculator for champion stats, levels, runes, items and customizations, with an action queue of sequential actions (max 60 actions or 60 seconds); totals include AD, AP, Armor and Crit. Not endorsed by Riot Games. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
