LeagueLab

LeagueLab is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Esports data and records. The site says it offers data-science articles, pro match archive, per-game esports charts and esports stats pages.

Visit leaguelab.cc

How the site describes itself

Korean-language League of Legends esports stats site: match results, drafts, scoreboards, objectives, gold curves and item builds, team pages, player rankings by position, champion stats and tier lists, pro solo-queue champion builds and weekly reports. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
