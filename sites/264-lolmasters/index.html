LoLMasters

LoLMasters is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Meta statistics. We saw champion tier list and live game companion.

Visit lolmasters.net

About

2016: meta analytics site with overall statistics, lanes meta, champion select, current-match lookup; showed patch 5.24 data from 8.56 million analysed picks. The live home page now says 'Project discontinued. Thank you all, it's been a blast.' (seen on the site, 2026-11-25)
