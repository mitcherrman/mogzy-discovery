LoLalytics

LoLalytics is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Meta statistics. The site says it offers rune and item recommendations, matchup statistics, champion win/pick/ban statistics and pro builds and pro pick stats.

Visit lolalytics.com

How the site describes itself

Win-rate analytics site: tier list, champion builds/runes/counters, ARAM and Arena tier lists, pro builds (Pros.LoL) and patch champion performance for Patch 16.23, with a stated Emerald+ default bracket and 'analyse 100% of champions in a bracket' method. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
