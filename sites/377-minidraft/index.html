MiniDraft

MiniDraft is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Draft tool. We tested its no account required, best-of series drafting, fearless draft support and observer links for drafts. The site says it offers optional account, ban phase support, champion pool tool and collaborative drafting. In one test session we ran 3 checks: 2 passed and 1 partly passed.

Visit minidraft.lol
