NeverFF

NeverFF is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Other. The site says it offers no account required, rune and item recommendations, counter-pick recommender and multilingual.

Visit neverff.com

How the site describes itself

Free independent site with League of Legends counter-build advisor, champion builds, counters, duo combos and tier lists from ~175,000 ranked EUW matches, plus World of Warcraft: Forever guides and an in-game leveling addon. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Site is shared with WoW Forever content; League section lives under /lol/ (counters, tiers, builds). (the site says, 2026-11-25)
