LoLSimulator

LoLSimulator is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Combat and damage calculator. The site says it offers champion-specific calculator, combo damage calculation and displays supported patch.

How the site describes itself

LoLSim, a League of Legends damage calculator/simulator. Each champion is individually implemented, so only some champions are available; includes a tutorial, a bug-report link and a known-issues list; app version 2.37.0, simulator version 3.32.2. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
