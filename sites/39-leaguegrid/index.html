LeagueGrid

LeagueGrid is a web game for League of Legends players, listed under Guessing game. The site says it offers no account required, archive of past daily puzzles, daily grid puzzle and daily reset puzzle.

Visit leaguegrid.net

How the site describes itself

Daily 3x3 League of Legends grid puzzle: fill each square with a champion matching its row and column categories; 9 guesses per grid, champions usable once. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
