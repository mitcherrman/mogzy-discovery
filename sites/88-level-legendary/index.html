Level Legendary

Level Legendary is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Map and strategy tools and Mechanics training. The site says it offers no account required, one-on-one coaching, map drawing/planning and mobile friendly (observed).

Visit levellegendary.com

How the site describes itself

Tools page of the Level Legendary coaching business ('2,500+ players coached', claim): a 3D Map Planner for drawing plays, wards, camps and ranges, and a Skillshot Trainer (Ezreal dodging with easy hitbox-visible and hard hidden modes). Smite Trainer and Draft Organiser listed as in development. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Audience as stated or implied by the page: League players who want to improve; TFT players also mentioned (the site says, 2026-11-25)
