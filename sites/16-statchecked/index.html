StatChecked

StatChecked is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Quiz and trivia. We tested its optional account, multiple daily modes, streaks or daily points and champion guessing. In one test session we ran 16 checks: 12 passed and 3 partly passed. The earliest public record we found dates from September 2026.
