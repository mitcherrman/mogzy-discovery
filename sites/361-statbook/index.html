StatBook

StatBook is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Esports data and records. We saw esports Elo ratings, esports stats pages and leaderboard. In one test session we ran 6 checks: 4 passed and 1 partly passed.

Visit statbook.lol

How the site describes itself

StatBook: 'LoL Esports Stats, Elo Rankings & History' (page title); navigation shows leaderboards including a GOAT leaderboard, Player Elo, Champion Elo, Player-Champion Elo, Team Elo, Region Elo and Recaps. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Professional League of Legends player, team and champion Elo rankings, career histories, GOAT leaderboards and tournament statistics. (the site says, 2026-11-24)
