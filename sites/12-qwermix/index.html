QWERmix

QWERmix is a web game for League of Legends players, listed under Daily game, Draft tool and Fan game. We tested its no account required, streaks or daily points, draft assistant / recommendations and composition archetypes. The site says it offers custom champion / augment creation. In one test session we ran 5 checks: 4 passed and 1 partly passed. The earliest public record we found dates from October 2026.

Visit qwermix.app
