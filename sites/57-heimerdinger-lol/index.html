Heimerdinger.lol

Heimerdinger.lol is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Cosmetics, skins and models. We saw no account required and multilingual. The site says it offers rank, LP and MMR calculators, skin collection tracker, mythic Shop / shop rotation tracker and skin, chroma, icon and emote database.
