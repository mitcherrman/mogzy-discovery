Draft Builder

Draft Builder is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Draft tool and Meta statistics. The site says it offers draft assistant / recommendations, ban phase support, champion pool tool and composition archetypes.

Visit draftbuilder.lol

How the site describes itself

Draft Builder: League draft tool with pick and ban suggestions, flex and comfort picks, comp types (Dive, Poke, Teamfight), a 'Squad' section for your mains, shareable drafts and keyboard shortcuts; not endorsed by Riot Games. (the site says, 2026-11-25)

Free League of Legends draft tool. Enter picks live in champ select and get counter picks, synergies and team comp advice from 120,737 ranked matches on patch 16.19. (the site says, 2026-11-24)
