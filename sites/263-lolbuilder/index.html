LoLBuilder

LoLBuilder is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Build tool. We saw shareable item builds and pro builds and pro pick stats.

Visit lolbuilder.net

Features

Build tool

  • Shareable item builds (seen on the site)

Meta statistics

  • Pro builds and pro pick stats (seen on the site)

Related sites

  • LoLTeam — Independent League of Legends statistics and build analysis site: champion builds (runes, items, skill orders, counters), tier list, ARAM and Arena builds, LCK schedule and standings, pro builds link. (Build tool, Esports data and records)
  • LoLvvv — Audience as stated or implied by the page: 'Beginners or an upcoming Pro' (Build tool, Esports data and records)
  • Invokers — Brazilian-Portuguese build, tier-list and guide site (BR server data): champions and tier list, pro builds, videos, an inhouse feature, lesson archive and a 'VIP' tier, plus a free Windows app that analyses your finished match. (Build tool)
  • LoLBuild.JP — Japanese-language build-guide search and posting site: user-written guides with weekly ranking and per-guide views and votes, champion statistics, 'ビルドナビ' and login/registration; guides tagged to patch labels (e.g. 26.22). (Build tool)
  • LoLMeta — Japanese-language stats site for the current meta: champion pick/ban rates (1/3/7/14/30-day windows), win rates, pro-match data and builds; archived pages show it updating through 2026. (Build tool, Meta statistics)
  • ProBuilds (Build tool)
