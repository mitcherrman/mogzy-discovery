ChampRival

ChampRival is a website for League of Legends players, listed under Quiz and trivia. We tested its no account required, difficulty levels, champion guessing and multilingual. The site says it offers daily reset puzzle, real-time multiplayer, tic-tac-toe duel and timed mode. In one test session we ran 2 checks: 1 passed and 1 partly passed.

Visit champrival.com

How the site describes itself

Free Spanish/English League of Legends champion trivia game: 30-second Contrarreloj, a daily champion, real-time 1v1 Champ Rival race vs friend, AI or random opponent, and a tic-tac-toe ability challenge. (the site says, 2026-11-25)
