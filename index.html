Mogzy Discovery: An independent atlas of League of Legends fan sites and tools

Mogzy Discovery is an independent atlas of League of Legends fan-made websites, tools and games: damage calculators, draft simulators, daily guessing games, stats trackers, esports archives and a lot more. It exists because many of these projects are made by one or two people and are hard to find.

Every listing comes from public pages and, for some categories, hands-on tests. Each fact shows how we know it, so you can tell what we tested, what we only saw, and what the site claims about itself. There is no paid placement and no ranking: browse by what you need, then judge for yourself.

276 fan-made sites, tools and games in the atlas; 29 tested hands-on across 192 checks.

  • League of Building (Combat and damage calculator, Build tool)

  • uDrafter — Free browser draft simulator and pick/ban tool that follows the official pick/ban order in real time, with Classic or Fearless series, shareable links with observer seats and a solo mode. Not affiliated with Riot Games. (Draft tool)
  • Outdraft.gg (Draft tool)
  • Games of Legends — Games of Legends: 'The home of League of Legends competitive statistics' with pro game statistics; sections Tournaments, Teams, Players, Champions, Plays, Tools and Premium, plus search by team, player or champion. (Esports data and records)

  • RiftWise — Pro game archive, match prep, records (Esports data and records)
  • EsportsValue — League of Legends esports hub: market values, stats, contracts, transfers and match schedule for pros; includes value movers, player comparison, watchlist, squad builder and daily games (Quizie, Higher or Lower, Guess the Value). (Esports data and records)
  • ChampRival — Free Spanish/English League of Legends champion trivia game: 30-second Contrarreloj, a daily champion, real-time 1v1 Champ Rival race vs friend, AI or random opponent, and a tic-tac-toe ability challenge. (Quiz and trivia)
  • Bonedle (Guessing game)
  • Trixcity — Esports history, schedules, player/team records; separate fantasy app (Esports data and records)
  • RuneGrove — Fan-run lore archive and hub for the Runeterra universe and Riot's unannounced MMO; also card/voice-line database, audio drama section, mini games, guides and tools. (Lore and encyclopedia)
  • LoLdoku — Independent free fan project: daily and unlimited League champion puzzle modes (Grid, Classic, Silhouette, Attributes, Abilities, Connections); not affiliated with Riot Games. (Daily game, Guessing game)
  • RiftCalc — Wiki-based calculations (Combat and damage calculator, Build tool)
  • Boris Diff — Boris Diff: League damage calculator; 'Build combos, compare damage, and theorycraft item builds'; champion picker currently lists Akali, Lux, Ahri, Katarina, Vayne, Jhin, Quinn and Locke with 'More champions coming soon' and a Discord vote for the next champion. (Combat and damage calculator)

  • HPMP — Per-hit combo playback (Combat and damage calculator)
  • LoL Damage Calculator — Combo and builds; planned enemy builder (Combat and damage calculator)
  • StatBook — StatBook: 'LoL Esports Stats, Elo Rankings & History' (page title); navigation shows leaderboards including a GOAT leaderboard, Player Elo, Champion Elo, Player-Champion Elo, Team Elo, Region Elo and Recaps. (Esports data and records)
  • League Of Data Base — Free multilingual League data reference built from Data Dragon, with any-patch switching, shareable item builds, trends and an API. (Patch notes and history, Lore and encyclopedia)
  • MiniDraft (Draft tool)
  • Champdle — Daily League quiz game with Quadra (grouping), Imposter (odd ability) and Guess-by-build modes plus player-made boards. (Daily game, Quiz and trivia)
